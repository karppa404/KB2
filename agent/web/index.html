Entry liquidity requirements

The T3 clock must be installed separately. Mode and starting capital are fixed after the first trade; use a new strategy to change them.

Entry / exit price rules

Entry triggers when the executable ask falls to or below your target. Take-profit triggers when the bid reaches your target; stop-loss when it falls to your stop. Exits require owned inventory. The exit limit is the observed bid minus the offset. Rules obey strategy pause and live-mode gates. They do not activate a schedule.

T3 native scheduling

Give this plan to your T3 agent and ask it to install the schedule with schedule_task. The one-minute clock checks strategy-local time, including daylight saving changes. Each tick starts a T3 turn and may incur model usage.

Turn off automatic reactivation per strategy to stop follow-ups. Pause a strategy to stop daily claims and new orders. Existing orders remain active until canceled or expired.
